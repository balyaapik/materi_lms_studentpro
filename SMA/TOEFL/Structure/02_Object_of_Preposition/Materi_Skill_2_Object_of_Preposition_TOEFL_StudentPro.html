Waspadai Object of Preposition

Jangan keliru menganggap noun setelah preposition sebagai subject utama kalimat.

PrepositionObjectSubject Finder

Tujuan Pembelajaran

Kamu akan mengenali frasa preposisi, menemukan object of preposition, dan memisahkannya dari subject utama—keterampilan penting ketika subject dan verb berjauhan.

PREPOSITION + NOUN / PRONOUN / GERUND / NOUN CLAUSE = PREPOSITIONAL PHRASE

1. Pengertian Object of Preposition

Object of preposition adalah kata atau kelompok kata yang hadir setelah preposition. Object tersebut melengkapi makna preposition, tetapi bukan subject utama dari klausa.

Bedah kalimat

After the final exam, the students will take a short break.

The final exam adalah object dari preposition after. Subject utama tetap the students; verb utamanya will take.

Catatan penting

Satu kalimat dapat berisi banyak frasa preposisi. Coret sementara semuanya ketika mencari subject dan verb utama.

(In the corner) (of the large room), a wooden cabinet stands.

3. Jebakan Subject Palsu

Noun yang paling dekat dengan verb belum tentu subject. Perhatikan batas frasa preposisinya.

Jebakan agreement

The quality of the products is improving.

Subject utama adalah quality (tunggal). Products hanya object dari of, sehingga tidak mengendalikan verb. Bentuk yang benar ialah is, bukan are.

KalimatSubject utamaVerb yang benar
The effects of the new policy ___ visible.effects (plural)are
A box of old photographs ___ upstairs.a box (singular)was found
The students in this class ___ prepared.students (plural)are

5. Strategi Cepat Mengerjakan Soal

  1. Lingkari preposition. Cari kata seperti of, in, at, with, from, before, dan after.
  2. Kurung frasanya. Tentukan object dan semua kata yang menerangkannya.
  3. Abaikan frasa itu sementara. Noun di dalamnya tidak boleh otomatis dianggap subject utama.
  4. Cari subject dan verb sejati. Periksa agreement berdasarkan subject, bukan object preposition.
  5. Masukkan kembali keterangannya. Pastikan arti kalimat tetap logis.
Ringkasan inti
  • Preposition diikuti object dan membentuk prepositional phrase.
  • Object of preposition tidak dapat menjadi subject utama dari klausa yang sama.
  • Pada pola noun + of + noun, noun pertama biasanya mengendalikan subject–verb agreement.
  • Gunakan object pronoun setelah preposition: for her, with them, between you and me.

6. Latihan Mandiri

1. With several senior researchers, _____ conducted the field study.

A. the new assistant
B. the new assistant was
C. during July
D. carefully
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: With several senior researchers adalah frasa preposisi. Conducted sudah menjadi verb, sehingga dibutuhkan subject the new assistant.

2. The condition of the laboratory equipment _____ checked every morning.

A. are
B. is
C. have
D. were
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Subject-nya the condition (tunggal). Equipment berada di dalam frasa of the laboratory equipment.

3. Before the opening ceremony _____ a brief rehearsal.

A. the performers held
B. held
C. the performers
D. in the hall
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Before the opening ceremony hanya keterangan. Kalimat utama memerlukan subject dan verb: the performers held.

4. The results from the two experiments _____ nearly identical.

A. was
B. is
C. were
D. has been
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Subject results berbentuk plural. Frasa from the two experiments tidak mengubahnya.

5. The confidential message was intended only for _____.

A. she
B. hers
C. her
D. herself was
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Preposition for harus diikuti object pronoun her.

6. In the last section of the report _____ three possible solutions.

A. discusses
B. the author discusses
C. the author
D. discussing
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Dua frasa preposisi mendahului klausa. Pilihan B memberi subject the author dan verb discusses.

7. A series of technical problems _____ the launch.

A. have delayed
B. delay
C. has delayed
D. delaying
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Head subject-nya a series (tunggal), sehingga bentuk yang tepat adalah has delayed.

8. By analyzing the samples carefully, _____ the source of contamination.

A. the source
B. was identified
C. the scientists identified
D. during the investigation
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Seluruh bagian by analyzing the samples carefully adalah frasa preposisi. Klausa utama memerlukan subject dan verb.
