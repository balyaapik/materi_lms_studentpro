TOEFL Structure · Skill 5

Waspadai Past Participles

Bentuk ketiga verb dapat menjadi bagian verb atau adjective—kenali auxiliary yang menyertainya.

Verb 3Perfect & PassiveAdjective

Tujuan Pembelajaran

Kamu akan mengenali bentuk past participle, membedakan fungsinya dalam perfect tense, passive voice, dan adjective phrase, serta menghindari anggapan bahwa setiap bentuk -ed adalah past-tense verb.

HAVE + V3 = PERFECT   |   BE + V3 = PASSIVE   |   V3 tanpa auxiliary = sering ADJECTIVE

1. Apa Itu Past Participle?

Past participle adalah bentuk ketiga verb (V3). Untuk regular verbs, bentuknya biasanya berakhir -ed. Irregular verbs memiliki perubahan yang beragam dan perlu dihafalkan.

JenisV1V2V3
Regularpaintpaintedpainted
Regularcompletecompletedcompleted
Irregularwritewrotewritten
Irregularchoosechosechosen
Irregularbuildbuiltbuilt

2. Tiga Fungsi Penting Past Participle

FungsiPolaContohAnalisis
Perfect tensehave/has/had + V3The family has purchased a television.Has purchased adalah verb phrase.
Passive voicebe + V3The poem was written by Paul.Was written adalah passive verb.
AdjectiveV3 + noun atau noun + V3 phraseThe poem written by Paul appeared in a magazine.Written ... menerangkan poem; verb utama appeared.
Bandingkan tiga fungsi

1. She has painted this picture. (perfect verb)

2. She was painted in this picture. (passive verb)

3. The picture painted by Sari is in a museum. (adjective)

Pada kalimat ketiga, painted by Sari tidak menjadi finite verb; finite verb-nya adalah is.

3. Past Tense atau Past Participle?

Pada regular verbs, V2 dan V3 memiliki bentuk sama. Fungsinya hanya dapat dipastikan melalui struktur kalimat.

KalimatFungsi completedAlasan
The team completed the project.Simple past verbBerdiri sebagai finite verb setelah subject.
The team has completed the project.Past participle dalam perfect verbDidahului has.
The project completed by the team won an award.Past participle sebagai adjectiveMenerangkan project; verb utama won.
Jebakan bentuk sama

The documents signed by the director yesterday.

Tanpa konteks lain, signed by the director terbaca sebagai adjective phrase dan kalimat tidak memiliki finite verb. Dua kemungkinan perbaikan:

  • The documents were signed by the director yesterday.
  • The documents signed by the director arrived yesterday.

4. Reduced Passive Adjective Clause

Past participle sebagai adjective sering berasal dari adjective clause pasif yang dipendekkan.

Perubahan bentuk

Lengkap: The packages that were mailed this morning will arrive Monday.

Reduced: The packages mailed this morning will arrive Monday.

That were dihapus. Frasa mailed this morning menerangkan packages, sedangkan verb utama ialah will arrive.

Makna pasif

Dalam reduced clause, noun biasanya menerima tindakan V3.

  • The books written in the nineteenth century are fragile. (books menerima tindakan write)
  • The bridge damaged by the storm remains closed. (bridge menerima tindakan damage)
  • The data collected from five cities support the hypothesis. (data menerima tindakan collect)

5. Present Participle vs Past Participle

Bentuk adjectiveMaknaContoh
V-ingNoun melakukan tindakan (aktif)The scientist conducting the study works here.
V3Noun menerima tindakan (pasif)The study conducted by the scientist was published.

6. Strategi Cepat Mengerjakan Soal

  1. Tandai bentuk V3. Waspadai regular -ed dan irregular seperti written, built, chosen.
  2. Cari auxiliary di depannya. Have membentuk perfect; be membentuk passive.
  3. Jika auxiliary tidak ada, cari noun yang diterangkan. Uji apakah V3 phrase bermakna pasif terhadap noun itu.
  4. Cari finite verb utama. Jika V3 hanya adjective, kalimat masih membutuhkan verb lain.
  5. Periksa agreement dan tense. Pilih is/are, was/were, has/have sesuai subject dan waktu.
Ringkasan inti
  • Past participle adalah V3; bentuknya tidak selalu berakhir -ed.
  • Have + V3 membentuk perfect tense; be + V3 membentuk passive voice.
  • V3 tanpa auxiliary sering berfungsi sebagai adjective atau reduced passive adjective clause.
  • V-ing adjective bermakna aktif; V3 adjective umumnya bermakna pasif.

7. Latihan Mandiri

1. The packages _____ at the central office will arrive tomorrow.

A. mailing
B. mailed
C. were mailed
D. have mail
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Will arrive sudah menjadi verb utama. Mailed at the central office adalah reduced passive adjective clause yang menerangkan packages.

2. The packages _____ at the central office yesterday.

A. mailed
B. were mailed
C. mailing
D. have mailing
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Kalimat belum memiliki finite verb. Karena packages menerima tindakan, gunakan passive verb were mailed.

3. The committee has _____ three proposals this month.

A. chose
B. choosing
C. chosen
D. choose
ANSWER: C
EXPLANATION: Setelah has diperlukan V3. Bentuk V3 dari choose adalah chosen.

4. The bridge _____ during the earthquake remains closed.

A. damaging
B. damaged
C. was damaged
D. has damage
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Verb utama remains sudah ada. Damaged during the earthquake menerangkan bridge dan bermakna pasif.

5. The final results _____ by an independent laboratory.

A. verified
B. were verified
C. verifying
D. have verify
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Tidak ada verb lain. Frasa by an independent laboratory menandai passive voice, sehingga diperlukan were verified.

6. The manuscript written in the seventeenth century contains detailed maps.

Fungsi written adalah ...

A. simple past verb utama
B. past participle sebagai adjective
C. present participle
D. auxiliary verb
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Written ... menerangkan manuscript. Verb utama kalimat adalah contains.

7. The data _____ from coastal areas have revealed a new pattern.

A. collecting
B. collected
C. were collected
D. have collect
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Have revealed adalah verb utama. Data menerima tindakan collect, jadi reduced passive adjective clause memakai collected.

8. _____ from recycled materials, the furniture is both durable and environmentally friendly.

A. Making
B. Made
C. Was made
D. It made
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: Made from recycled materials adalah introductory past-participial phrase yang menerangkan the furniture dengan makna pasif.
